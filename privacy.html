BadDrivr

Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

BadDrivr is operated by Xuberent Technologies. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what happens to it. The short version: we collect what the record needs and nothing for advertising. We do not sell data and we do not track you across other apps.

What we collect from you

Account data. Your email address, a user id, and your chosen username. Sign in is by email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In. We receive only the identity token those providers issue; we never see your Apple or Google password.

Precise location. When you file a report, the incident's location pins the report to a place and powers the map and heat surfaces. Location is collected only while you use the app, with your permission, and only to attach to reports and center your map. We do not track your location in the background.

Video you submit. The clip you file is uploaded to our servers, where automated redaction obscures license plates before a public version exists. Frames from the clip are also processed by automated safety screening before publication.

Comments, flags, blocks, and Hawks. Comments you post, content you flag, users you block, and your Hawks balance and transactions.

Data about third parties

Reports contain license plate text and footage of vehicles on public roads. That is information about a vehicle, and indirectly its registered owner, not about the app user who filed it. We treat it accordingly:

  • Plate text is stored masked and is displayed as stars to viewers who are not entitled to see it.
  • Plates in video are redacted server-side before any clip becomes public. Clips fail closed: no redaction, no publication.
  • Reports must not identify people. Content that does is removable under our Terms of Service, and every report and comment carries a flag control that routes to human review.

How we use data

  • To operate the service: hosting reports, rendering the map and feed, running comments and Hawks.
  • To keep the service safe: automated screening of new content, redaction, moderation of flagged content, rate limiting, and enforcement against abuse.
  • To communicate service matters to you at your account email.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising. We do not sell or rent personal data. We do not use your data to track you across apps or websites, and the app contains no advertising SDKs.

Who processes data for us

We use a small set of infrastructure providers to run the service: a hosted backend and database (Supabase), video processing infrastructure for redaction, and an AI provider (Anthropic) whose model detects license plates in frames during redaction and safety screening. These providers process data solely to provide the service to us under their data processing terms.

Legal disclosures

We disclose data when the law requires it. Apparent child sexual abuse material is reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children as required by federal law, and material connected to such a report is preserved as the law requires, including through account deletion. We may also disclose data in response to valid legal process.

Retention and deletion

  • You can delete any of your reports in the app; the public clip and thumbnail are removed.
  • You can delete your account in Settings. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, reports, clips, comments, and Hawks are removed, except records we are legally required to preserve as described above.
  • Operational logs are retained briefly for security and debugging and then expire.

Your choices

  • Location permission can be denied or revoked in iOS Settings; reports then file without your current position.
  • You control what you film and what you submit.
  • You can block any user in the app and manage blocks in Settings.
  • For access or deletion requests beyond the in-app tools, contact us at the address below.

Children

BadDrivr is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will announce material changes to this policy in the app before they take effect.
