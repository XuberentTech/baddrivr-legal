Bad driving, on the record. Plate, not person.

BadDrivr is an iOS app for documenting unsafe driving. Reports are about vehicles and license plates, never about people. Plates are masked in the app and redacted in every public clip before anyone sees it.

Get help

Questions, problems, or requests about your account or data: email xuberenttechnologies@gmail.com. We respond to support and moderation matters within 24 hours.

Report content

Every report and comment in the app carries a flag control that routes to human review. You can also block any user from the same menus and manage blocks under Settings. To report content by email, include a link or description and we will review it within 24 hours.

Your data

You can delete any of your reports in the app, and you can delete your whole account under Settings. Deletion is permanent. For anything the in-app tools do not cover, email us.
